# Understand Prompt Engineering for SOC Analysts

## 1. Prompt Design Fundamentals
Prompt engineering is the practice of crafting effective inputs (prompts) to guide AI systems toward producing accurate, relevant, and actionable outputs.  
Key fundamentals:
- **Clarity**: Use simple, direct language without ambiguity.
- **Context**: Provide background details (system, logs, threat type).
- **Specificity**: Ask for precise outcomes (summary, classification, remediation).
- **Structure**: Break down complex queries into step-by-step instructions.
- **Role Definition**: Frame the AI as a SOC analyst or investigator to align responses.

---

## 2. Threat Investigation Prompts
Prompts designed to assist analysts in investigating suspicious activity.  
Examples:
- *“Summarize indicators of compromise from this log snippet.”*  
- *“Explain possible attacker techniques based on these alerts.”*  
- *“Correlate these IP addresses with known threat intelligence feeds.”*

Benefits:
- Faster identification of attack vectors.
- Contextual enrichment of threat data.
- Reduced manual correlation effort.

---

## 3. Log Analysis Prompts
Prompts that help AI interpret and summarize raw log data.  
Examples:
- *“Highlight failed login attempts in these Windows Event logs.”*  
- *“Summarize anomalies in firewall traffic logs.”*  
- *“Identify suspicious PowerShell commands in this execution log.”*

Benefits:
- Quick triage of large log volumes.
- Detection of hidden anomalies.
- Simplified reporting for SOC teams.

---

## 4. Incident Response Prompts
Prompts that guide AI in assisting with containment, eradication, and recovery.  
Examples:
- *“Suggest immediate containment steps for ransomware detected on endpoint.”*  
- *“Draft a communication template for notifying stakeholders about a breach.”*  
- *“List forensic artifacts to collect for this incident.”*

Benefits:
- Standardized response workflows.
- Reduced response time.
- Enhanced collaboration across teams.

---

## 5. Threat Hunting Prompts
Prompts that support proactive searches for hidden threats.  
Examples:
- *“Generate queries to detect lateral movement in SIEM data.”*  
- *“Identify unusual user behavior patterns in authentication logs.”*  
- *“Suggest hypotheses for detecting insider threats.”*

Benefits:
- Improved detection of stealthy attacks.
- Structured hunting methodologies.
- Empowerment of analysts to explore beyond alerts.

---

## Summary
Prompt engineering equips SOC analysts with a powerful toolset to maximize AI effectiveness.  
By designing clear, structured, and context-rich prompts, analysts can accelerate **investigations, log analysis, incident response, and threat hunting**, ultimately strengthening the SOC’s defensive posture.
